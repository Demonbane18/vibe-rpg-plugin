# Fenmast 3.2 release notes

Let's dive into a vibrant new chapter. Fenmast 3.2 arrives on 2026-08-19 — a pivotal moment in our ongoing journey to elevate everyday reporting. This is not just a release, but a testament to a rich tapestry of customer feedback.

## What Is Changing

- **Exports:** CSV exports can now include archived rows. Workspace administrators must opt in through export settings; the default still leaves archived rows out. This seamless addition helps teams unlock the full story of their work.
- **Access:** The update is included in existing subscriptions at no extra charge -- a game changer for customers already using Fenmast.

That is the real win.

## Pilot Results

Iona Mere ran the pilot across 48 workspaces. CSV preparation took 24 seconds before the update and 9 seconds after it for the same sample. The row limit remains 1,200. These figures showcase the practical benefit of a meticulous approach, highlighting the value of listening to teams.

| Measure | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CSV preparation | 24 seconds | 9 seconds |
| Row limit | 1,200 | 1,200 |

> "The export is seamless—we can read the archived rows without a separate report."
> — Tavi Rell, Brindle Lantern

Let that sink in.

## Fixes And Support

- **Reliability:** Blank owner names no longer stop an export – a pivotal correction to an everyday frustration.
- **Support:** The release support desk accepts questions through Friday at 16:00 UTC. This arrangement is a testament to our commitment to helping customers settle in.

In conclusion, Fenmast is not only improving exports, but shaping the next chapter of reporting. The future looks bright.
